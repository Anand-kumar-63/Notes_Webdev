## 4. Positioning & Centering (MOST IMPORTANT)
### `fixed`
- The dialog stays centered even when the page scrolls.
### `left-1/2 top-1/2`
`left: 50%; top: 50%;`
Moves the **top-left corner** of the dialog to the center of the screen.
### `-translate-x-1/2 -translate-y-1/2`
`transform: translate(-50%, -50%);`
- Pulls the dialog back by **half of its own width and height**
- This is the classic **perfect centering technique**
