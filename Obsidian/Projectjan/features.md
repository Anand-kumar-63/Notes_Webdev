## 1. Core Objective (Very Important)

The **Alumni–Student module** exists to solve **three real problems**:
1. Students do not have access to **credible guidance, referrals, or funding**
2. Alumni want to **give back**, but only in a **structured, low-noise way**
3. Investors/alumni want **filtered talent**, not random DMs    

So this **must NOT behave like Instagram/LinkedIn DMs**.

---

## 2. User Roles in This Module

### A. Student
- Requests mentorship
- Applies for funding/guidance
- Books sessions
- Consumes verified alumni content

### B. Alumni
- Offers mentorship / referrals / funding
- Sets availability & pricing
- Chooses domains of expertise
- Accepts or rejects requests

### C. Platform (System Role)
- Verifies alumni identity
- Manages payments
- Enforces boundaries
- Prevents spam

---

## 3. Alumni Profile (Structured, Not Social)
This is **not a normal user profile**.


**Mandatory Fields**
- Name (verified)
- Graduation year & college
- Current company / startup
- Role & experience level    
- Domains (e.g., Backend, Product, Finance, AI)

- Mentorship type offered:
    - Career guidance
    - Resume review
    - Mock interviews
    - Startup funding
    - Referrals

**Trust Signals**
- Verified alumni badge
- Session count
- Student ratings
- Response rate

---
## 4. Student → Alumni Interaction Flow (Critical)
### Step 1: Discovery
Students browse alumni via:
- Domain filters
- Company filters
- Graduation year
- Funding/mentorship type
### Step 2: Request (Controlled Access)
Student **cannot directly DM**.
Instead:
- Select purpose (Mentorship / Funding / Referral)
- Short proposal form:
    - Why you?
    - What help do you need?
    - Duration & urgency
### Step 3: Access Gate (Monetization Point)
Before request is sent:
- Pay **₹250 platform fee** (as you mentioned earlier)
- OR consume monthly free credits (if allowed)

This:
- Filters unserious users
- Respects alumni time    
- Generates revenue

---
## 5. Alumni Response Flow

Alumni can
- Accept
- Reject
- Ask for clarification

On accept:
- Chat opens OR
- Session booking enabled (calendar-based)
- Optional video call integration later

---
## 6. Funding & Investor Angle (Advanced Layer)

This is where it becomes **powerful**.
### Student Startup Pitch Section
- Short pitch deck upload
- Idea summary (problem, solution, traction)
- Funding requirement
### Alumni / Investor View
- Browse pitches by domain
- Save promising startups
- Request follow-up meetings
This **must be private**, not public feed content.

---
## 7. Anti-Spam & Quality Control (Non-Negotiable)
- Daily request limits per student
- Minimum profile completeness to request
- AI-assisted proposal quality check
- Alumni can block/report students

---

## 8. Monetization Strategy (Clean & Ethical)

|Feature|Monetization|
|---|---|
|Mentorship request|₹250 per request|
|Premium alumni access|Subscription|
|Funding pitch visibility|Paid boost|
|Corporate alumni hiring|B2B plans|

---
## 9. Why This Module Will Actually Work
Most platforms fail because:
- No boundaries
- Free spam access
- No incentive alignment

Your design:
- **Respects alumni time**
- **Filters students**
- **Creates real value exchange**
    This makes alumni **stay**, not churn.

---
## 10. MVP Scope (What to Build First)
If you are smart about MVP
1. Alumni verification
2. Structured profiles
3. Paid mentorship request flow
4. Acceptance/rejection system
5. Chat after acceptance
Everything else is Phase-2.
